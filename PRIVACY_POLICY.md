# WheelBridge – Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 13, 2026

## 1. Introduction

WheelBridge is a free, open-source Windows application developed and maintained by MattyDrex71.

This Privacy Policy explains how WheelBridge handles user information.

## 2. Data Collection

WheelBridge does not intentionally collect personal information, usage statistics, analytics, or telemetry.

The application does not require users to create an account or provide personal information.

## 3. Local Data Storage

WheelBridge stores application settings and controller profiles locally on the user's computer.

These files are stored in the Windows Local Application Data folder:

`%LocalAppData%\WheelBridge\Profiles`

This information is used to save user preferences and controller configurations.

## 4. Internet Connectivity

WheelBridge does not intentionally send personal information or usage data to external servers.

No online account or cloud service is required for the application's core functionality.

## 5. Third-Party Components

WheelBridge uses third-party components:

- **Nefarius.ViGEm.Client** – used to manage the virtual Xbox 360 controller.
- **Vortice.DirectInput** – used to communicate with DirectInput-compatible devices.
- **ViGEmBus** – a separate driver that may be installed by the WheelBridge installer.

These components are developed and distributed by their respective maintainers. Their own behavior and privacy practices are outside the direct control of WheelBridge.

## 6. Data Security

Application settings and profiles are stored locally on the user's computer.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their device and local files.

## 7. Children's Privacy

WheelBridge does not intentionally collect personal information from children or other users.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when WheelBridge's features, dependencies, or data-handling practices change.

The latest version will always be available in the official GitHub repository.

## 9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or WheelBridge, please open an issue in the official GitHub repository:

https://github.com/MattyDrex71/WheelBridge
